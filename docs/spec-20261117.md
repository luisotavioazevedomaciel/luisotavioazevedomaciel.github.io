# Specification: Services & Generic Contact Page (`EN` & `PT`)

## 1. Contact Page (`/contact/` & `/pt/contato/`)

### 1.1 Purpose & Routing
* **Single Generic Entry Point:** `/contact/` (`<html lang="en">`) and `/pt/contato/` (`<html lang="pt-BR">`) serve as a single, general-purpose contact page for **any** inquiry—consulting, mentoring, technical leadership opportunities, or general outreach.
* **Zero Query-Parameter Complexity:** All CTAs across the site link directly to `/contact/` or `/pt/contato/` without track-specific URL parameters, conditional forms, or backend dependencies.
* **Locale Mapping:** Registered in `src/i18n/routeMap.ts` (`/contact/` ↔ `/pt/contato/`) with matching `canonical` and `hreflang` (`en`, `pt-BR`, `x-default`) tags.

### 1.3 Interactive & Footer Requirements
* **Unboxed Contact Actions:** Do not place the email or LinkedIn actions inside a card or bordered container. Keep the email icon/label and its actions first, with LinkedIn directly below it at every viewport size. Separate the compact groups with a subtle horizontal divider.
* **Clean Contact Display:** Do not show the raw email address or LinkedIn URL as visible text. Keep the email address as the copy value and `mailto:` target; the LinkedIn row label and icon identify its full-row link.
* **Email Actions:** Show `Copy` / `Copiar` and `Send email` / `Enviar e-mail` actions side by side in the email row. The send action is an anchor to `mailto:otavioazevedo7@gmail.com`.
* **One-Click Copy:** Place a sibling `<button type="button">` (`Copy` / `Copiar`) inside the email row (never nested inside `<a>`) using `navigator.clipboard.writeText()` with a 2-second `Copied!` / `Copiado!` state and `aria-live="polite"` announcement.
* **Contact Icons:** Include recognizable email and LinkedIn icons in their respective rows. Treat decorative icons as hidden from assistive technology; the text labels and actions provide the accessible names.
* **LinkedIn Row:** Make the full LinkedIn row a single anchor linking to `https://www.linkedin.com/in/luisotavioazevedomaciel/` (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`) and displaying `linkedin.com/in/luisotavioazevedomaciel`.
* **Footer Anchoring:** Include `© 2026 Luís Maciel` in the dark footer bar alongside the email and LinkedIn icon links. On short pages, keep the footer at the viewport bottom using page layout flow; do not fix or overlay it.

## 2. Services Page (`/services/` & `/pt/servicos/`)

### 2.1 Page Structure
* **Locale Mapping:** `/services/` (`<html lang="en">`) ↔ `/pt/servicos/` (`<html lang="pt-BR">`).
* **Audience Separation:** Visually and structurally separate **Track 1: B2B Consulting** from **Track 2: B2C Mentoring** so teams and individual engineers immediately recognize their path.
* **Conversion Path:** Both tracks route directly to the generic `/contact/` (`EN`) or `/pt/contato/` (`PT`) page.
* **UI Boundaries:** No public pricing tables, discount badges, star ratings, placeholder testimonials, or embedded third-party booking widgets.

---

### 2.2 Track 1: B2B — Engineering & Architecture Consulting

* **Positioning:** Helping engineering teams design, modernize, and build reliable software systems through independent architectural guidance and practical technical assessments.
* **Target Audience:** CTOs, technical founders, engineering managers, startups, scale-ups, and teams modernizing legacy systems.
* **Service Areas:**
  1. **Software Architecture & System Design:** Architecture reviews, system design, distributed systems, API/integration strategies, trade-off analysis, and Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).
  2. **Cloud Engineering & Infrastructure:** Cloud architecture assessments, Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices, migration planning, and cost, security, and reliability reviews.
  3. **Application Modernization:** Legacy system assessments, incremental migration strategies, pragmatic monolith decomposition, and technical debt prioritization.
  4. **DevOps & Reliability Engineering:** CI/CD improvements, deployment strategy reviews, observability, resilience practices, operational readiness, and developer experience.
  5. **Engineering Advisory:** Technical decision support, engineering process reviews, documentation standards, and technical leadership support.
* **Initial Engagement Packages:**

| Package | Scope | Concrete Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Architecture Review** | Independent evaluation of an existing system or proposed architecture. | Risk & bottleneck analysis, trade-off evaluation, prioritized recommendations. |
| **Technical Assessment** | Targeted diagnostic of an architecture, cloud/IaC, or delivery challenge. | Current-state diagnostic and actionable improvement roadmap. |
| **Engineering Advisory** | Recurring technical guidance for ongoing architecture decisions and initiatives. | Regular architecture/design discussions and technical leadership support. |

* **CTA:** `Get in touch` (`/contact/`) | `Entrar em contato` (`/pt/contato/`).

---

### 2.3 Track 2: B2C — Software Engineering Mentoring

* **Positioning:** Practical 1:1 mentoring for software engineers focused on technical growth, leadership readiness, and international career transitions.
* **Credibility Anchor:** Grounded in 7+ years of software engineering and Technical Lead experience across distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, production reliability, internal engineering standards (Engineering Handbook, Tech Radar), and firsthand international relocation.

#### Mentoring Groups
1. **Group 1 — Career Growth Mentoring**
   * **Target Audience:** Individual contributors progressing from junior roles to team-level technical leadership.
   * **Career Stages:**
     * **Junior → Mid-level:** Becoming an autonomous contributor through clean code, maintainable design, automated testing, API/database fundamentals, debugging, and structured delivery habits.
     * **Mid-level → Senior Engineer:** Mastering system design trade-offs, distributed architecture, cloud and Infrastructure-as-Code practices, production observability, incident triage, and written technical proposals (ADRs/RFCs).
     * **Senior Engineer → Tech Lead:** Shifting from individual delivery to team-level technical leadership—facilitating design decisions, aligning with Product Owners and stakeholders, mentoring peers, and balancing technical quality with delivery.
   * **Core Deliverables:** Annotated code or architecture reviews, mock system design or leadership scenarios, and a structured 30-60-90 day growth roadmap.

2. **Group 2 — International Engineering Career Mentoring**
   * **Target Audience:** Software engineers pursuing cross-border roles or relocation into international engineering teams.
   * **Focus Areas:**
     * Positioning CVs and professional profiles for international recruiters and hiring standards.
     * Preparing for multi-stage technical and behavioral interview loops in English.
     * Communicating architectural decisions and trade-offs clearly in cross-cultural, distributed teams.
   * **Core Deliverables:** Line-by-line English CV and profile rewrite, plus a targeted international job-search and interview readiness checklist.
   * **Scope Disclaimer:** Practical career experience sharing only—does not constitute legal, tax, visa, or immigration advice.

#### Engagement Formats (Continuity Progression)
| Tier | Format | Cadence & Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Entry** | **Diagnostic Session** | Single 60-min live session + async pre-review + written action plan. |
| **2. Kickoff** | **4-Week Focus Sprint** | 4 × 60-min weekly sessions + async check-ins + 30-day milestone plan. |
| **3. Core Continuity** | **Bi-Weekly Growth Retainer** | 2 × 60-min sessions/month + async reviews (month-to-month, pause/cancel anytime). |
| **4. Low-Touch** | **Async Continuity Retainer** | Async code/ADR/CV reviews + 1 × 30-min monthly check-in. |

* **Strict Mentoring Boundaries:**
  * Grounded strictly in demonstrated Technical Lead experience (no Staff or Principal Engineer coaching claims).
  * No guarantees of job offers, visa approvals, promotions, or salary outcomes.
  * No ghostwriting take-home coding tests or live interview assistance.
* **CTA:** `Get in touch` (`/contact/`) | `Entrar em contato` (`/pt/contato/`).
